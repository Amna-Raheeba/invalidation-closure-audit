# Headline results

Across 54 seeded update events on belief graphs of 60/150/300 beliefs (max depth 5, 24.8 beliefs affected per update), explicit-negation-only memory leaves 93.7% of affected beliefs silently stale - and 97.5% of those at depth >= 3. Dependency closure over the store's recorded edges cuts deep staleness to 26.4% (oracle closure over the complete edge set: 0.0%, so the remainder is a provenance-logging gap, not a policy gap). The recency window spends 15.8 recomputations per update for almost nothing at depth (95.9% deep staleness): it repairs only beliefs whose entire chain back to the fact sits inside its window, and leaves the rest touched but still wrong. The aggressive cluster flush is freshest inside its cluster but is the only policy that breaks beliefs the update never touched: collateral damage 3.48% of unaffected beliefs. Cost per update: negation-only 3.3 recomputations, recency 15.8, closure 29.2, flush 29.2.
